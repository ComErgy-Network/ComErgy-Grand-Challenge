# ComErgy Grand Challenge

**Designing Fair, Intelligent and Resilient Community Energy Systems**

Welcome to the official repository of the ComErgy Grand Challenge, an international initiative of the [ComErgy Network](https://comergy.com.au).

The Grand Challenge brings together researchers, practitioners and innovators to develop, evaluate and share approaches to community energy systems using common benchmarks.

## Our objectives

- Promote energy justice, fairness and equitable participation in community energy systems.
- Enable transparent, reproducible and comparable research through shared benchmarks.
- Encourage international collaboration and knowledge exchange.
- Support open innovation by enabling researchers to learn from and build upon previous contributions.

## Grand Challenge 2026

The inaugural Challenge focuses on a community of nine households with solar PV, battery storage and electricity market arrangements.

This repository will host the official benchmark, evaluation methodology and voluntarily shared solutions from participating teams.

## Contributing

We welcome contributions from Grand Challenge participants and the wider research community. Contributions should respect intellectual property rights, properly acknowledge authors and include appropriate documentation and licensing.
